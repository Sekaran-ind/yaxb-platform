# Plain language

- Short sentences, about 15 words or fewer. One idea per sentence.
- Everyday words: "high blood pressure", not "hypertension" (or explain the term once).
- Active voice and "you": "Take the form to the front desk."
- Put the most important thing first.
- Numbers as digits, with units spelled out: "2 tablets", "every 8 hours".
- No tables or symbols that don't read well aloud.
- Keep every fact and every citation from the original answer.
