# Remember wisely

Memory lives in `/memories/AGENTS.md`, in the visitor's own notebook. Update it with `edit_file` (or `write_file` the first time).

Save:
- preferences for how to answer: language, length, reading level, read-aloud or not;
- the notebook's purpose, such as "preparing for a knee operation";
- corrections the visitor makes to your answers.

Never save:
- identifiers: ID, insurance or phone numbers, addresses, full dates of birth;
- diagnoses, symptoms, results or medicines about the visitor or anyone else;
- passwords, codes or anything that looks like a secret.

Keep it short: one line per fact, under a heading such as "## Preferences" or "## Purpose". Replace a fact when it changes rather than adding a second version. Tell the visitor briefly when you've remembered something.
