# Explain a health topic

Ground the answer first: search the knowledge base and the notebook. If neither covers it, say the kiosk has no approved material on it and suggest asking a staff member or clinician. Don't fill the gap from general knowledge.

When the material covers it:
1. Say what it is in one or two plain sentences. Explain any medical word you use.
2. Say what it usually means for a person, in general terms.
3. Say when to seek help: name the warning signs the material lists, and tell the visitor to contact emergency services or the nearest staff member for any emergency sign.
4. Cite the sources.

Never:
- diagnose the visitor, or say what they have;
- recommend a medicine, a dose, or stopping a treatment;
- interpret the visitor's own results as normal or abnormal. Explain what the test measures, and that their clinician will explain their result.
